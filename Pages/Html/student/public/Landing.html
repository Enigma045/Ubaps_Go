Empowering Your Academic Journey at UNILIA

The University of Livingstonia Bursary Application & Processing System (UBAPS) is dedicated to supporting students who demonstrate excellence and financial need.

Eligibility Criteria

Ensure you meet these core requirements before applying for the UNILIA Bursary.

📚

Academic Standing

Strict "No Repeat" policy. Eligibility is reserved for students who have never received a repeat course or a repeat semester throughout their studies.

🔝

Priority Levels

While all students can apply, priority is given to Level 2 students and onwards during the selection process.

🏫

Institutional Scope

This bursary program is exclusively available for registered students of the University of Livingstonia.

How to Apply

Follow these simple steps to secure your financial support.

1

Create Your Account

Register using your student credentials and log in to access the secure student portal.

2

Submit Application

Complete and sign the digital bursary application form with accurate personal and financial details.

3

Await Selection

Your application will undergo a multi-stage review by Deans and the Registrar. Check your status in the dashboard.

4

Thank Your Benefactor

If selected, you are required to write a formal thank you letter to your benefactor expressing your gratitude.

5

Final Submission

Send your thank you letter through the dedicated Student Letter page to finalize your bursary award.
